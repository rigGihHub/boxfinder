# DrakenDavids catalogue import

The complete public Shopify catalogue was collected on 15 October 2026. Pagination returned 250, 250, 71 and 0 products, with unique product and variant IDs: 571 products and 1,784 variants.

All variants enter the catalogue review inventory, including accessories, singles and break slots. Explicit sealed-card formats create 338 offers, of which 83 were available and 255 unavailable. Stock is the observed Shopify variant availability; it is not a promise of stock at checkout. No preorder notices were present in this snapshot.

Break slots, selected singles, supplies, figures and puzzles do not become opening recommendations. Products whose format or randomized contents cannot be established remain in review. Known DrakenDavids offers reuse their existing SKU, canonical product and analysis identities, including the Disney Chrome 2026 Value Box (now 499 SEK).

New products have no invented pack counts, chase profiles, hit odds, card values or scores. They appear in the selected-store discovery catalogue, with a direct variant URL and observation date. Only separately reviewed content profiles qualify for ranking. The ranking page links to this catalogue without a price ceiling; store-specific catalogues request up to 200 products, sufficient for all 83 currently available offers.

The compressed source snapshot is bundled with the backend and imported at startup. This is a one-time observation, not a live stock feed. Refresh with `scripts/snapshot_drakendavids_once.py`, review changes, then replace the snapshot. Existing explicit review rejections are preserved.
